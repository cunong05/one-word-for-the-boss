> Slacktator

The idea: a boss who rules not from an office but from Slack — every
decision, directive, and 11pm "quick question" arrives as a message.
Part dictator, part channel tyrant, all ping. It lands on its own because
everyone who has worked under one knows the feeling instantly: the boss
isn't in the room, he's in the thread.
